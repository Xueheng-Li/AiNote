# Research Quality Criteria

Guidelines for evaluating source quality and research comprehensiveness.

## Source Evaluation Tiers

### Tier 1: Highest Authority (Prioritize)
- Peer-reviewed academic papers (journals, conference proceedings)
- Official government reports and statistics
- Reports from reputable research institutions (Brookings, RAND, NBER)
- Primary sources and original datasets

### Tier 2: High Authority (Include)
- Reputable news outlets (academic focus)
- Industry white papers from established organizations
- Books from recognized experts
- Official documentation from authoritative bodies

### Tier 3: Moderate Authority (Use with Caution)
- Blog posts from recognized experts
- Wikipedia (as starting point only, cite underlying sources)
- Press releases (verify claims independently)
- Trade publications

### Tier 4: Low Authority (Avoid)
- Anonymous or unverified sources
- Content farms or SEO-optimized articles
- Social media posts (unless from verified official accounts)
- Outdated sources (>5 years for fast-moving topics)

## Minimum Quality Thresholds

| Metric | Requirement | Fallback Action |
|--------|-------------|-----------------|
| Total sources | 10+ | Mark as incomplete if < 5 |
| Tier 1 sources | 3+ | Document gap in limitations |
| Recent sources (<2 years) | 5+ | Note temporal limitations |
| Cross-verification | Key claims verified in 2+ sources | Flag unverified claims |

## Research Depth Indicators

**Comprehensive (Target)**:
- 1500+ words of substantive analysis
- 3-5 distinct subtopics covered
- Multiple perspectives represented
- Quantitative data included where applicable
- Clear synthesis and implications

**Adequate**:
- 800-1500 words
- 2-3 subtopics
- Main perspectives covered
- Some supporting data

**Incomplete**:
- < 800 words
- Single perspective
- Missing key subtopics
- Limited supporting evidence

## Language Balance

For bilingual research (Chinese/English topics):
- If topic is China-specific: 60% Chinese, 40% English sources
- If topic is international: 40% Chinese, 60% English sources
- Always include both perspectives when relevant
- Note any language-based gaps in coverage

## Recency Requirements

| Topic Type | Recency Requirement |
|------------|---------------------|
| Technology/AI | 1-2 years (fast-moving) |
| Social science theory | 5-10 years acceptable |
| Current events | 6 months or less |
| Historical topics | Seminal works + recent analysis |
| Policy/regulation | Check for latest updates |
